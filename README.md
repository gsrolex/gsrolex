### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm Ammon, a developer from Norway. I started in web, and for the last two years I've been building a game on my own, start to finish: design, code, art direction, audio, the Steam page, all of it.

I like systems that let players break them on purpose. Most of my time goes into making numbers go up in ways that feel earned.

<br>

### 🎮 Now building: SpinTactics

A roguelike engine builder. You spin, trigger symbols, make combos, and build an engine out of Companions and Gems. Between rounds you grow the engine until every spin scores bigger than the last. Every run ends up a different machine.

- Built in Unity / C#, one developer, ~2 years
- Free demo live on Steam, full release coming soon
